Unlike sprawling mega-hotels where guests are just room numbers, Hotel Grand Godwin's 39 boutique rooms provide quiet elegance, personal warmth, and unmatched accessibility.

Positioned just 500 meters (5 minutes walk) from the New Delhi Railway Station (Ajmeri Gate) and 700 meters from the Airport Express Metro, our guests bypass Delhi’s notorious traffic jams and arrive with ease.

Guest Clarifications

Hotel Grand Godwin FAQs

Answers to common traveler questions regarding our 39-room boutique hotel, location, and services.

How many rooms does Hotel Grand Godwin have?

Hotel Grand Godwin features exactly 39 boutique rooms and suites. This intimate capacity ensures uncrowded hallways, zero lift congestion, and personalized hospitality from our dedicated team.

What are the room categories and prices?

We offer three room categories:
• Deluxe Room (240 sq ft) — Starting from ₹2,800/night
• Executive Club Room (320 sq ft) — Starting from ₹3,800/night
• Grand Family Suite (480 sq ft) — Starting from ₹5,200/night

How far is the hotel from New Delhi Railway Station?

Hotel Grand Godwin is just 500 meters (approx. 5 minutes walk) from the Ajmeri Gate exit of New Delhi Railway Station (NDLS) and 700 meters from the Airport Express Metro Station.

Is Coffee Brownie cafe open 24 hours?

Yes! Coffee Brownie is our on-site 24-hour boutique cafe serving fresh brews, espresso, teas, sandwiches, and pastries round the clock for late-night arrivals and early morning departures.

What are the check-in and check-out timings?

Standard check-in is at 1:00 PM (13:00) and check-out is at 11:00 AM (11:00). Complimentary early check-in from 10:00 AM is offered subject to room availability upon arrival. 24-hour secure luggage storage is always complimentary.
