Hotel Godwin Deluxe FAQs

Answers to common traveler questions regarding our 26-room designer boutique hotel, air purification, and location.

How many rooms does Hotel Godwin Deluxe have?

Hotel Godwin Deluxe features exactly 26 designer boutique rooms. This boutique capacity ensures attentive service, uncrowded common areas, zero elevator waiting, and quiet relaxation.

What are the 4 room categories available?

We offer 4 distinct room categories:
• Standard Room (Ground Floor) (220 sq ft, Queen Bed) — Starting from ₹2,800/night
• Premier King Room (280 sq ft, Comfort King Bed) — Starting from ₹3,400/night
• Premier Twin Room (280 sq ft, 2 Separate Twin Beds) — Starting from ₹3,400/night
• Premier Studio King Room (450 sq ft, Master King with Velvet Lounge Sofa) — Starting from ₹4,800/night

Are air purifiers provided in all rooms?

Yes! All 26 rooms at Hotel Godwin Deluxe are equipped with high-performance HEPA air purifiers, ensuring you enjoy pure, clean, allergen-free air during your stay in Central Delhi.

How far is Hotel Godwin Deluxe from New Delhi Railway Station?

Hotel Godwin Deluxe is located at 8501/15 Arakashan Road, just 500 meters (approx. 5 minutes walk) from the Ajmeri Gate exit of New Delhi Railway Station (NDLS) and 700 meters from the Airport Express Metro Station.

What is The Indian Grill restaurant?

The Indian Grill is our premier multi-cuisine restaurant located right on the ground floor of Hotel Godwin Deluxe. Serving gourmet North Indian Mughlai curries, clay oven kebabs, Continental, and daily hot breakfast buffet from 7:30 AM to 10:30 AM.
