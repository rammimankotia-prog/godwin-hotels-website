Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Direct answers to the most common questions travelers ask about Hotel Grand Godwin and Hotel Godwin Deluxe in New Delhi.

Both hotels are located on Arakashan Road, Ram Nagar, Paharganj, New Delhi 110055.

Hotel Grand Godwin is at 8502/41 Arakashan Road and Hotel Godwin Deluxe is at 8501/15 Arakashan Road. Both are situated right next to each other, just 500 meters (5 minutes walking distance) from New Delhi Railway Station (Ajmeri Gate exit) and 1.2 km from Connaught Place.

Hotel Grand Godwin provides classic warm hospitality and spacious family suites, while Hotel Godwin Deluxe features sleek modern designer styling and executive studio rooms.

Both properties are managed by the same hospitality team and share high standards of cleanliness, elevator access, 24/7 security, The Indian Grill restaurant, and rooftop terraces.

Both hotels are only 500 meters from New Delhi Railway Station and 700 meters from the Airport Express Metro Station.

You can easily walk to both the train platforms and the New Delhi Airport Express Metro station, which reaches Indira Gandhi International Airport (IGI) in just 22 minutes.

Check-in is at 13:00 (1:00 PM), and check-out is at 11:00 (11:00 AM) at both hotels.

Early check-in from 10:00 AM is available complimentary subject to room availability upon arrival. Luggage storage is available free of charge at reception for early arrivals or late train departures.

Yes, both hotels feature The Indian Grill restaurant, Coffee Brownie cafe, and scenic rooftop terraces.

The Indian Grill serves freshly cooked Indian, Continental, Chinese, and Italian cuisines, along with a daily morning breakfast buffet. The rooftop terrace provides city views and open-air seating.

Yes, round-the-clock private airport transfers and Delhi / Agra sightseeing can be arranged via the travel desk.

Our 24-hour front desk can arrange chauffeured airport pickups with a placard greeting from IGI Airport, as well as day tours to the Red Fort, Qutub Minar, Lotus Temple, and the Taj Mahal in Agra.

No, pets are not allowed in guest rooms or dining facilities, with the exception of certified service animals.
