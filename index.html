Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Direct answers to the most common questions travelers and booking engines ask about Godwin Hotels & Resorts.

No, Godwin Hotels does not permit pets in guest rooms or dining areas, with the exception of certified service animals.

To maintain hygiene, allergen-free suites, and comfort for all our patrons, pets are strictly prohibited across all Godwin properties in Haridwar, Meerut, and Goa. Certified guide dogs and assistance animals accompanying guests with disabilities are welcomed.

Check-in is at 14:00 (2:00 PM), and check-out is at 11:00 (11:00 AM) across all Godwin Hotels properties.

Early check-in from 10:00 AM is available complimentary subject to room availability upon arrival. Guaranteed early morning check-in requires reserving the room from the preceding night. Late check-out until 14:00 may be requested at reception and is subject to occupancy.

Yes, a complimentary lavish buffet breakfast is included in all Bed & Breakfast room rates.

Breakfast is served daily between 07:00 AM and 10:30 AM at The Golden Palm restaurant, offering a comprehensive spread of Indian, Continental, and American breakfast dishes, fresh juice bars, and live egg stations.

Godwin Hotel Haridwar is located 6.5 km from Har Ki Pauri (12 minutes drive) and 8.2 km from Haridwar Railway Station.

The property is conveniently positioned on the main NH-58 Rishikesh Highway opposite Shantikunj, providing easy highway transit away from the crowded city center while remaining close to the holy bathing ghats and Rishikesh (18 km).

Godwin Hotel Meerut accommodates up to 1,500 guests in the Kohinoor Ballroom and up to 2,500 guests on the royal lawns.

The property is widely recognized as Western UP's premier wedding venue, featuring a completely pillar-less grand ballroom, separate bridal dressing suites, expansive parking for 500+ cars, and custom catering menus curated by master chefs.

Godwin Hotel Candolim Goa is situated 700 meters from Candolim Beach, an easy 7-minute walk.

Guests can walk comfortably to the beach or take advantage of the hotel's complimentary golf-cart / beach shuttle service. The property is also 4.2 km from Fort Aguada and 3.5 km from Calangute Beach.

Reservations can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours prior to scheduled check-in.

Cancellations made within 48 hours of arrival, or no-shows, will incur a fee equal to the first night's room charge plus applicable taxes. Non-refundable promotional rates are non-cancellable once confirmed.

Yes, all Godwin Hotels provide free on-site secure parking with 24/7 valet service, plus EV charging at Godwin Meerut.

Both self-parking and round-the-clock valet services are completely complimentary for all staying guests and event attendees. Godwin Meerut is equipped with fast EV charging stations compatible with all major electric vehicle models.
