Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to the most common questions about Hotel Grand Godwin and Hotel Godwin Deluxe in New Delhi.

Both hotels are on Arakashan Road, Ram Nagar, Paharganj, New Delhi 110055.
Hotel Grand Godwin is at 8502/41 and Hotel Godwin Deluxe is at 8501/15 — right next to each other, just 500 metres (5-minute walk) from New Delhi Railway Station (Ajmeri Gate exit) and 1.2 km from Connaught Place.
Hotel Grand Godwin offers classic warm hospitality and spacious family rooms; Hotel Godwin Deluxe offers sleek modern designer boutique styling.
Both are managed by the same hospitality team and share the same high standards: immaculate rooms, 24/7 security, elevator access, The Indian Grill restaurant, and rooftop terraces.
500 metres from NDLS Railway Station and 700 metres from the Airport Express Metro Station.
The New Delhi Airport Express Metro reaches Indira Gandhi International Airport (IGI) in just 22 minutes. Perfect for early flights or late arrivals.
Check-in is at 13:00 (1 PM); check-out is at 11:00 AM at both hotels.
Early check-in from 10:00 AM is complimentary subject to availability. Luggage storage is always available free of charge for early arrivals or late departures.
Yes — The Indian Grill restaurant (Godwin Deluxe), Coffee Brownie 24h café (Grand Godwin), and scenic rooftop terraces at both hotels.
The Indian Grill at Hotel Godwin Deluxe serves freshly cooked Indian, Continental, Chinese, and Italian cuisines with a daily breakfast buffet. Coffee Brownie café at Hotel Grand Godwin is open 24 hours. Both hotels also have open-air rooftop terraces.
Free cancellation up to 24–48 hours before check-in, depending on your rate tier.
Standard bookings can be modified or cancelled free of charge outside the window. Cancellations within 24-48 hours of arrival incur a one-night room charge.
