Historic Marvels

The Iconic Monuments in Delhi

Delhi has stood as the imperial seat of seven historic empires. From towering sandstone minarets to magnificent Mughal garden tombs, explore Delhi's crown heritage monuments.

UNESCO Heritage 3.8 km from Hotel
9:30 AM – 4:30 PM Closed Mondays

Red Fort (Lal Qila)

Constructed by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in 1638 when he shifted the empire's capital from Agra to Delhi. Featuring massive 33-meter high red sandstone octagonal ramparts, the monumental Lahori Gate, Diwan-i-Aam (Hall of Public Audience), and the jewel-like Diwan-i-Khas.

  • Must-See: Lahori Gate, Pearl Mosque & Evening Light & Sound Show
  • Distance: ~15 mins private cab drive from Godwin Hotels
  • Photography: Spectacular early morning exterior light

7:00 AM – 5:00 PM Open All Days

Qutub Minar Complex

The world’s tallest brick minaret standing at 72.5 metres, initiated by Qutb-ud-din Aibak in 1192. Marvel at intricately carved Quranic inscriptions, the ancient 1,600-year-old rust-resistant Iron Pillar of Chandragupta II, and the elegant Alai Darwaza gateway.

  • Highlight: 4th-century metallurgical marvel Iron Pillar
  • Setting: Lush green lawns and ancient stone arches
  • Tips: Perfect afternoon visit with beautiful shadow patterns

UNESCO Heritage 7.5 km from Hotel
6:00 AM – 6:00 PM Open All Days

Humayun's Tomb

Built in 1570 by Empress Bega Begum, this grand Persian-Mughal architectural masterwork served as the direct aesthetic inspiration for the Taj Mahal in Agra. Situated within a serene 30-acre Charbagh (four-quadrant) garden divided by bubbling water channels.

  • Key Architecture: First garden tomb on Indian subcontinent
  • Reflection Pool: Incredible mirror reflections at midday
  • Atmosphere: Peaceful, uncrowded, pristine manicured grounds

India Gate & Kartavya Path

A soaring 42-metre triumphal sandstone arch designed by Sir Edwin Lutyens, commemorating 84,000 soldiers of the British Indian Army who lost their lives in World War I. Located along the freshly redeveloped Kartavya Path (Central Vista) overlooking Rashtrapati Bhavan.

  • Highlights: National War Memorial & Amar Jawan Jyoti flame
  • Best Time: Sunset & evening illumination with fountain lights
  • Vibe: Strolling lawns, ice cream vendors, patriotic spirit

Lotus Temple (Bahá'í House of Worship)

A landmark of modern architecture shaped like a blossoming lotus flower with 27 free-standing marble-clad 'petals' arranged in clusters of three. Welcoming visitors of all faiths, religions, and backgrounds to meditate in total silence and harmony.

  • Entry: Free admission for all guests; silent prayer hall
  • Ponds: 9 serene reflection ponds surrounding petals
  • Dress Code: Modest attire; shoes deposited safely at gate

Jama Masjid (Old Delhi)

One of India’s largest and most magnificent historic mosques, commissioned by Shah Jahan in 1650. Constructed of red sandstone and pure white marble, its expansive courtyard accommodates over 25,000 worshippers, with 40-metre minarets offering bird's-eye Old Delhi panoramas.

  • Highlight: Climb the Southern Minaret for Old Delhi views
  • Proximity: Directly opposite Chandni Chowk street entrance
  • Culinary Note: Located next to famous Karim’s and Al Jawahar

Vibrant Markets & Street Flavours

The Famous Historic Bazaars in Delhi

Delhi’s centuries-old bazaars are a riot of sensory wonder — sacks of fragrant cardamom, rows of handcrafted silver jhumkas, vibrant silk saris, and century-old sweet shops.

ASIA'S LARGEST SPICE MARKET
Old Delhi Heritage Market

Chandni Chowk & Khari Baoli Spice Bazaar

Dating back to 1650 and designed by Princess Jahanara Begum, Chandni Chowk ("Moonlit Square") is the beating heart of Old Delhi. Only 2 km from Godwin Hotels Paharganj, explore the fragrant aroma of saffron and whole spices at Khari Baoli, intricate silver at Dariba Kalan, and shimmering bridal wear at Kinari Bazaar.

Famous Street Food Delicacies to Try:
  • Hot, stuffed paranthas at the historic Paranthe Wali Gali (since 1872)
  • Crispy, syrup-soaked jalebis at Old Famous Jalebi Wala (since 1884)
  • Soft lentil dumplings and spiced yogurt at Natraj Dahi Bhalla Corner (since 1940)
Visit with ₹4,500 Day Cab Just 10 mins rickshaw ride from hotel

Dilli Haat (INA Crafts Market)

Open-Air Rural Craft Village · South Delhi

An authentic permanent thatched-roof craft village where certified master artisans from every Indian state rotate fortnightly. Shop genuine Pashmina shawls, Madhubani paintings, bronze sculptures, rosewood carvings, and taste authentic regional cuisines from Nagaland momos to Rajasthani dal baati.

10:30 AM – 10:00 PM • Entry Fee: ₹100 (Foreigners) / ₹30 (Indians)

Janpath & Connaught Place

Only 1.2 km from Godwin Hotels · Central Delhi

Just minutes from our Paharganj hotels, explore Janpath's Tibetan market street lined with brass deities, turquoise necklaces, embroidered cushion covers, and vintage leather journals. Continue to Connaught Place’s grand Georgian colonnades, heritage bakeries (Wenger’s), and high-end Indian designer flagship showrooms.

Walkable / 5 mins cab • Open Mon–Sat 11:00 AM – 8:30 PM

Sarojini Nagar & Central Market

Fashion Bargains & Traditional Textiles

Delhi's premier shopping destination for export apparel, bohemian dresses, sunglasses, and tote bags. Across the city, Lajpat Nagar’s Central Market is famous for stunning bridal unstitched fabrics, Chikankari Kurtas, mehendi (henna) artists, and hot Ram Laddoos with radish garnish.

Closed Mondays (Sarojini Nagar) • Bargaining expected
Must-Visit Gems

Unmissable Places to See in Delhi

Beyond the famous sandstone ramparts lie tranquil 15th-century gardens, mystical stepwells, and magnificent spiritual complexes.

Lodhi Gardens & Art District

A 90-acre lush oasis featuring the 15th-century tombs of Mohammed Shah and Sikandar Lodi, the Bara Gumbad, and Shisha Gumbad. Nearby Lodhi Colony showcases India’s first open-air public street art district with monumental wall murals painted by global artists.

6 km from Godwin Hotels • Free Entry

Agrasen ki Baoli Stepwell

A 60-meter long ancient stepwell with 108 stone steps descending down into four tiered arched levels. Hidden right off Hailey Road near Connaught Place, this 14th-century architectural relic is a photographer's dream for dramatic geometric shadows.

2.8 km from Godwin Hotels • Open daily 9am–5pm

Swaminarayan Akshardham

Carved from Rajasthani pink sandstone and Italian Carrara marble without any structural steel. Includes a 10,000-year Indian cultural boat ride, the Sahaj Anand water musical fountain show, and sprawling Yagnapurush Kund gardens.

Closed Mondays • Strict electronics ban

Raj Ghat & Gandhi Smriti

A simple, deeply touching black marble memorial marking the cremation site of Mahatma Gandhi on the banks of the Yamuna River, adorned with an eternal flame. Nearby Gandhi Smriti preserves the Father of the Nation's final living quarters and footsteps.

4 km from Godwin Hotels • Serene park setting

Delhi Sightseeing & Tour FAQs

Common questions answered by the Godwin Hotels travel desk team.

What is included in the ₹4,500 INR Delhi local tour?

The ₹4,500 package includes an exclusive, private air-conditioned sedan car for up to 8 continuous hours and up to 80 kilometers of driving. It covers all fuel costs, all parking fees, all toll taxes, and the chauffeur's allowance. Doorstep pick-up and drop from the lobby of Hotel Grand Godwin or Hotel Godwin Deluxe is included.

Are monument entrance tickets included in the ₹4,500 fare?

No, monument entrance tickets are not included. This is designed for your flexibility, allowing you to choose whether you want to enter a monument or simply photograph its grand facade. You can purchase tickets directly at official ASI ticket counters or online via your smartphone.

Which monuments are closed on Mondays?

The Red Fort and the Lotus Temple are closed to visitors every Monday. However, Qutub Minar, Humayun’s Tomb, India Gate, Jama Masjid, Bangla Sahib Gurudwara, and all bazaars remain open. If your tour falls on a Monday, our travel desk customizes your route so you still experience a rich full-day itinerary.

Can we customize the stops and itinerary?

Absolutely! Since this is a 100% private car tour (not a shared bus tour), you have total control over the pace, start time, and sights you wish to prioritize. If you wish to spend more time shopping in Dilli Haat or Chandni Chowk, simply let your chauffeur know.

What happens if our tour exceeds 8 Hours or 80 Kilometers?

If you decide to extend your sightseeing beyond the package limits, extra hours and kilometers are billed at nominal, standard government-aligned rates (approx ₹200 per extra hour and ₹16 per extra km). Everything is recorded transparently on your duty slip.
